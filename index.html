Privacy Policy for Lyrix Write & Record Studio

Last updated: 11/15/2025

Lyrix Write & Record Studio (“the App”) is developed by Encore Applications, owned by Jack Douglas (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our Android application.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

Lyrix Write & Record Studio does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, if you contact our support team).

1.2 User-Generated Content

This includes, but is not limited to:

  • Lyrics you write
  • Audio recordings you create
  • Saved projects, titles, and drafts

All user-generated content is stored locally on your device. We do not upload, collect, or access your content on our servers. It remains private to you unless you choose to export or share it.

1.3 Audio Recording

The App requires microphone access to allow recording. We only access your microphone when you choose to start a recording. All recordings are stored on your device only and are never sent to us.

1.4 Device Permissions

To function properly, the App may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone Access – used only for recording vocals or audio.
  • Storage Access – to save lyrics, audio files, and project data.
  • Internet Access (optional) – may be used for non-personal features such as app updates, license checks, or crash reporting.

The App does not access photos, contacts, messages, or any other personal data on your device.

1.5 Crash Logs & Diagnostics (Optional)

If crash reporting or diagnostics are enabled through Google Play services, basic non-identifiable information (such as device model, operating system version, and error details) may be collected automatically. This data is used only to improve app stability and fix bugs. We do not collect personal information through diagnostics.

2. How We Use Information

Any information the App interacts with is used only to:

  • Provide core features (editing lyrics, recording audio, saving projects)
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Troubleshoot crashes or bugs
  • Provide customer support if you contact us directly

We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to any third parties.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • All lyric files, recordings, and user content remain on your device.
  • We do not store or maintain copies of your data on cloud servers.
  • If you uninstall the App, all locally stored data is removed from your device unless you exported it elsewhere.

We follow standard security practices for handling any optional, non-identifiable crash data.

4. Children’s Privacy

Lyrix Write & Record Studio is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe any data has been collected unintentionally from a child, please contact us so we can remove it.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use trusted third-party tools such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase Crashlytics (optional)

These services may collect limited diagnostic data strictly for app improvement purposes.

For more details on how Google handles data, please review Google’s Privacy Policy .

6. Your Rights & Control

Because your content is stored entirely on your device, you maintain full control over:

  • Deleting lyrics or recordings
  • Resetting or clearing app data
  • Uninstalling the App at any time

Uninstalling the App will delete all stored data from your device.

7. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or applicable laws. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
